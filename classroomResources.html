Classroom Resources

Season One: Episode One "The Golden Stool"

Suggested audience: high school students (grades 9-12), adaptable for introductory college courses

Guiding Questions: To what extent do women's protests against British imperial injustice reshape our understanding of their role in the making of colonial laws and political discourse? In what ways do such resistances challenge enduring narratives of female passivity in society?

Relevant Common Core standards:

Key concepts

  • • Imperialism: The extension of one country's political, economic, or cultural power over another.
  • • War: A period during which a state or group is engaged in open armed conflict against another state or group.
  • • Protest: The expression of strong disapproval or opposition to something, including a law or policy.
  • • Tax: The imposition of money by an authority on a person or property to fund public services.
  • • Solidarity: An agreement between and support for members of a group to pursue a common interest.
  • • Authority: A legitiamte power that influences thorugh, opinion, and behavior.
  • • System: A means of working or doing things based on a given plan or set of rules.
